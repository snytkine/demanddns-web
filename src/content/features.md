# Features

Command DNS brings the DNS server for your local network and its administration together in one application.

## Graphical administration

Manage everything from a GUI. Add zones and records, change settings and review activity without editing configuration files by hand.

## Local names for local devices

Give workstations, servers, printers and services on your network memorable names, and keep those names under your own control.

## Fast answers from cache

Answers are cached on your network segment, so repeated lookups are served locally instead of travelling to the Internet every time.

## Upstream forwarding

Names that Command DNS does not know are forwarded to the upstream resolvers you choose. Only those queries leave your network.

## Query visibility

See which devices are asking for which names, so you can understand and troubleshoot what is happening on your network.

## Locally hosted

Command DNS runs on hardware you own, on your own network. Your DNS configuration and query history stay with you.
